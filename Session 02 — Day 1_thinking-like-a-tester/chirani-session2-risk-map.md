## Deliverable: 3 — Risk Map with a Working Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This report ranks potential risks by likelihood and impact and links each risk to existing test conditions in Deliverable 2. The file data/risk-register.csv is the source of truth. The ranked table and results summary are generated by python3 tools/score_risks.py.

 ## 2. Scoring Method

Likelihood (L): 1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High.

Impact (I): 1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High.

Risk Score: Likelihood × Impact.

Risks are ranked by score, then impact.

## 5. Why I Scored the Top Three This Way

R-02 — Tutor cancellation: Likelihood is 3 because the founders' statements conflict about cancellation timing and refunds, making inconsistent implementation likely until the policy is resolved. Impact is 3 because incorrect decisions can cause disputes, lost bookings, and financial harm.

R-01 — Payments: Likelihood is 2 because network retries are a plausible integration failure, though not inevitable. Impact is 3 because duplicate charges directly affect a student's money and trust.

R-03 — Payment/booking integration: Likelihood is 2 because gateway success and booking confirmation can become inconsistent across systems. Impact is 3 because a student may be charged without receiving a confirmed session.

## 6. If I Had One Hour

1. TC-59 — Payment retries after network timeout: Check that retrying a payment does not cause a duplicate charge. This is a high-impact financial risk.
2. TC-09 and TC-60 — Payment and booking integration: Check the successful-payment booking outcome and the case where payment succeeds but booking confirmation fails.
3. TC-16, TC-17 and TC-18 — Tutor cancellation: Check cancellation more than 12 hours before, less than 12 hours before, and exactly 12 hours before the scheduled session start, once the product owner confirms the policy.
4. TC-19, TC-20, TC-21 and TC-63 — Commission and payouts: Check commission calculation, the tutor's net amount, weekly payouts, and the agreed handling of cancelled or refunded sessions.
5. TC-35, TC-36, TC-37 and TC-62 — Privacy and child safety: Check consent capture, deletion requests, data-protection handling and prevention of unauthorised access to personal data.

## 7. Risks I Am Deliberately Not Testing in V1

Azure architecture preference (R-15): Defer detailed deployment validation while Azure remains a preference rather than a final decision. The product owner or technical lead should accept this temporary deferral and confirm the target architecture.

Detailed visual polish for ratings (R-10): Perform a smoke check for rating eligibility and valid values, but defer detailed visual polish because payment, cancellation, privacy, and booking integrity have greater potential impact. The product owner should accept this limited scope.

Extended video-call quality beyond the required concurrency (R-13): First validate the selected provider integration and the 200-session target. Plan longer-duration quality testing after the product owner and technical lead agree on acceptance thresholds.

## 8.How to Run the Checks

Run these commands.

**Check test-condition coverage:** 

python3 tools/check_coverage.py

**Check and rank the risks:**

python3 tools/score_risks.py

Both scripts should finish with exit code `0`.

The coverage script checks that there are at least 25 test conditions, at least 8 negative or boundary conditions, and coverage of all required functional requirements, non-functional requirements, and constraints.

The risk script checks for at least 10 risks, at least 3 risks with scores of 6 or 9, 100% of risks linked to existing test-condition IDs, and at least 3 distinct risk-score values.
